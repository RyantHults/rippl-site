Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 17, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to rippl, a self-hosted social media management application. This privacy policy explains our approach to data collection and storage.

Data Collection

We do not collect or store any user information.

rippl is designed as a self-hosted application, which means:

  • All data is stored on your own infrastructure
  • No data is transmitted to our servers
  • We have no access to your information
  • You maintain complete control over your data

Development Status

rippl is currently in active development. As the application evolves, we remain committed to the principle of self-hosting and user data privacy. Any future changes to this privacy policy will be clearly communicated.

Self-Hosted Nature

Because rippl is self-hosted:

  • You are responsible for securing your own installation
  • Your data privacy depends on your hosting environment
  • We recommend following security best practices when deploying rippl
  • You control who has access to your data

Third-Party Services

When you use rippl to connect to social media platforms, you will interact directly with those platforms' APIs. Please refer to each platform's privacy policy for information about how they handle your data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or rippl's approach to data privacy, please visit our GitHub repository or contact us through the project's issue tracker.
